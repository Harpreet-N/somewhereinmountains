Privacy policy

Short version: this website uses no cookies, no analytics and no tracking, and our forms don't send your data anywhere on their own. You decide what to share when you message us on WhatsApp.

1. Who is responsible

Jenish Maharjan (contact details in the legal notice above) is responsible for processing your personal data in connection with this website and your trip. We follow the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) for travellers from the EU and Nepal's Individual Privacy Act 2075 (2018).

2. Visiting this website

When you open a page, your browser sends technical data to the server that hosts this website (for example your IP address, date and time, the page requested and your browser type). This is necessary to deliver the page and keep it secure. This website is currently hosted on claude.ai by Anthropic. Fonts, icons and images are served from the same server — no requests go to Google or other third parties.

3. Trip and review forms

The “Plan your trip” and “Write a review” forms work entirely in your browser. If you email us instead, we use your message only to answer you and plan your trip. They turn what you type into a WhatsApp message — nothing is sent or stored until you open WhatsApp and press send yourself. Once you send it, the message is handled by WhatsApp (Meta Platforms Ireland Ltd. / WhatsApp LLC) under WhatsApp's privacy policy.

4. When you book a trip

To organise your trip we need your name, contact details, travel dates, passport details and photo (for trekking permits and the TIMS card), and your travel insurance details. Legal basis: carrying out your booking (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR) and legal requirements in Nepal (Art. 6(1)(c)). We pass permit data to our partner trekking agency and the Nepali authorities that issue permits, and booking details to lodges, rafting or safari operators only as far as needed for your trip.

5. Health information on the trek

If you tell us about medical conditions, and during the daily altitude check-ins (oxygen saturation, heart rate, blood pressure), we process health data. We do this only with your explicit consent (Art. 9(2)(a) GDPR), use it only for your safety on the trip, and pass it on only in an emergency (for example to a doctor or rescue service). Readings are deleted [within 30 days after the trek]. The check-ins are a safety routine, not a medical diagnosis.

6. Photos, films and trail logs

Photos and videos we take of you on the trek, and your personal trail log, are made for you. We publish photos in which you can be recognised — on this website or social media — only if you agree, and you can withdraw that agreement at any time.

7. Reviews

We publish a review only if you ticked the box to allow it, showing only your first name, country, trip and month. Ask us anytime and we'll remove it.

8. How long we keep data

We keep booking data for as long as needed for your trip and as required by Nepali tax and tourism rules, then delete it. Messages you no longer need us to keep can be deleted on request.

9. Your rights

You can ask for access to your data, correction, deletion, restriction of processing, a copy of your data (portability), and you can object to processing or withdraw consent at any time. Just message us on WhatsApp or email jenishmaharjan26@gmail.com. If you are in the EU, you can also complain to the data protection authority in your country (in Austria: the Datenschutzbehörde).

Booking terms

These terms apply to treks, rafting trips, safaris and other services booked with Jenish Maharjan. Your written quote on WhatsApp — with itinerary, price and what is included — is part of the agreement.

1. Who organises your trip

Jenish Maharjan is your guide and point of contact. Under Nepal's trekking rules, trekking permits and the TIMS card are issued through a government-registered trekking agency; for this we work with [partner agency name]. Rafting and safari activities are run by licensed local operators, whose own safety rules also apply.

2. Booking and confirmation

You send your dates and wishes on WhatsApp; we reply with a day-by-day itinerary and a fixed price. Your booking is confirmed once you accept the quote in writing and we receive the deposit.

3. Price and payment

The quote lists exactly what is included (for example guide, permits, transport, accommodation, meals) and what is not. A deposit of [x %] confirms the booking; the balance is due [on arrival in Kathmandu / x days before departure], payable in [cash in USD/NPR, card, bank transfer].

4. If you cancel

Please tell us as early as possible, in writing on WhatsApp. Cancellation charges: [more than 30 days before: deposit refunded minus costs already paid] · [15–30 days: …] · [less than 15 days / no-show: …]. Permits, flights and other costs already paid on your behalf can only be refunded where the provider refunds them.

5. Changes for safety, weather or closures

Mountains and rivers decide the plan. If weather, landslides, river levels, closures, strikes or flight cancellations make the itinerary unsafe or impossible, we change the route or schedule. Where we have to cancel before departure, you can choose an alternative trip or a refund of the amount paid minus costs that can't be recovered. Extra costs caused by events outside our control (for example extra nights after a cancelled Lukla flight) are not included.

6. Health, fitness and altitude

Please tell us before booking about any medical conditions or medication that matter at altitude or on the river. The guide may change the pace, add a rest day, or decide that you must stop ascending or descend if your health or the group's safety requires it. That decision is final, and it doesn't entitle you to a refund for the part of the trip you miss.

7. Travel insurance

Travel insurance is required. It must cover trekking up to the highest altitude of your trip, helicopter rescue and evacuation, medical treatment and — if you raft — white-water rafting. Please send us your policy details before the trip.

8. Your responsibilities

You need a valid passport and Nepal visa, suitable clothing and gear (we help you check it before departure), and to follow the guide's safety instructions, local laws and customs, and the rules of national parks and conservation areas.

9. Risk and liability

Trekking at altitude, rafting and wildlife safaris carry risks that can't be fully removed, even with careful planning. We are liable for damage we cause intentionally or through gross negligence, and for injury to life, body or health caused by our fault. Otherwise, our liability is limited to the price of the trip, as far as the law allows. We are not responsible for services you book directly with third parties.

10. Photos and films

Photos, films and trail logs we make for you are for your personal use. We share pictures of you only with your permission (see the privacy policy).

11. Complaints

If something isn't right, tell your guide straight away so it can be fixed on the spot. Anything else, message us on WhatsApp after the trip and we'll reply within [14] days.

12. Applicable law

The law of Nepal applies. If you are a consumer living in the EU, this doesn't take away the protection given to you by the mandatory laws of your home country.
