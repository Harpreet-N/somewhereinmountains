Itinerary

Jenish sends a day-by-day itinerary with your quote, with walking times, overnight stops and timings planned around your pace. A typical 2-day trip is the starting point; it can be shortened, stretched or combined.

Difficulty and fitness

Easy

Short days on good paths. If you are active and can walk a few hours with breaks, you'll be fine.

Good for: Anyone who wants a night by the river.

Best season

Best: Oct–May. Weather in the mountains changes fast and every season has trade-offs, so we choose dates together.

Riverside tents, sleeping mats and all meals. Your quote lists exactly where you sleep and which meals are included.

Included and not included

Included

Not included

  • International flights and your Nepal visa

  • Everything else, such as meals, transport and porters, is listed in your written quote

Preparation and safety

Bring clothes that can get wet, sun cream and a change for the evening. Helmets, life jackets and paddle jackets are provided.

Licensed local operators run the rafting and safari activities, with helmets and life jackets on the river. Their safety rules apply, and the river is only run when water levels allow.

Price

Request a personalised quote. Every trip is priced for your group. The price depends on group size, number of days, season, permits and transport. Your written quote lists what is and isn't included, and the booking terms explain deposits and cancellations.
