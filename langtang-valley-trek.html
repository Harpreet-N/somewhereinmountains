Langtang Valley Trek

The closest big-mountain trek to Kathmandu — no flight needed. Tamang villages, yak pastures and glaciers above Kyanjin Gompa.

Duration
7 days
Level

Highest point
Highest point 3,870 m (4,773 m optional)
Best season
Oct–Dec, Mar–May

Highlights

  • Optional climb of Kyanjin Ri for a 360° glacier view
  • Local cheese factory and Tamang heritage
  • Starts with a scenic drive from Kathmandu

Included with every trek

  • Morning and evening oxygen and heart-rate checks
  • An edited highlight film and your digital trail log
  • A trail comfort pouch with ginger-lemon-honey, masala tea and electrolytes
  • Gear check, SIM and money help the day before
