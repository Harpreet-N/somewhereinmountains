# Website audit — Somewhere in Mountains

Audit of the site as it was on 24 November 2026 (commit `218b963`, live at
https://somewhereinmountains.vercel.app), before the redesign in this repository.

## 1. What the site was

- **Stack:** static HTML/CSS/JS, generated by a small Python script (`source/build.py`) from
  one content file (`source/data.py`). No framework, no backend, no tracking. Fonts
  (Newsreader, Instrument Sans) and Lucide icons self-hosted. Deployed on Vercel (Hobby team).
- **Pages (14):** home, tours, contact, legal, and 10 trip pages (7 treks, 2 Trishuli rafting
  trips, 1 rafting + Chitwan safari combination).
- **Inquiry:** a contact form that turned the fields into a WhatsApp message; WhatsApp links on
  every page; a floating WhatsApp bubble. Reviews were sent the same way and published by
  editing `data.py`.
- **Strengths kept:** authentic photography by Jenish, a clear voice in his own words, the
  "summit marker" altitude detail, the season chart, accessible accordions and carousel,
  0 axe violations at the time, no third-party requests.

## 2. Usability and journey issues

| # | Issue | Where |
|---|---|---|
| U1 | No way to find a suitable trek without reading every page — no filters beyond activity type, no comparison, no guidance for undecided visitors | tours.html |
| U2 | The guide, the main differentiator, appeared only halfway down the home page; no dedicated profile page | index.html |
| U3 | The process after contact ("what happens next") was never explained; no statement that an inquiry is not a booking | site-wide |
| U4 | Only WhatsApp was offered for the inquiry form; no email path, no copy option | contact.html |
| U5 | Trek preselection relied on a `#trip-id` hash; no validation message or fallback text | contact.html |
| U6 | Review form occupied half the home page, competing with the inquiry | index.html |
| U7 | Trip pages had no itinerary, difficulty, accommodation, included/excluded, safety or price sections | trip pages |
| U8 | Main navigation used "Treks & tours / Reviews / Contact"; the primary action was WhatsApp rather than planning | header |
| U9 | Mobile menu button text switched to upper case ("MENU"/"CLOSE") | site.js |
| U10 | FAQ had 8 questions; cost, booking, gear, accommodation, date changes and travel to Nepal were missing | index.html |

## 3. Design consistency

- Spacing values were mostly on a 4px grid but undocumented; no spacing tokens.
- Two different card patterns for trips (photo / no-photo) depending on whether photos existed.
- "What comes with every trek" listed paid-looking extras (food walk, Ayurvedic massage)
  as if included in every trek.
- Footer contact email broke mid-word at some widths.

## 4. Accessibility (WCAG 2.2 AA)

- axe-core: 0 violations on the old build. Lighthouse flagged nav text over the hero as low
  contrast on trip pages (a false positive caused by a transparent header with no scrim, but a
  real risk on bright photos).
- Form field borders 1.4:1 and empty rating stars 1.2:1 (below the 3:1 non-text contrast
  minimum) — fixed in commit `218b963` before the redesign.
- Trek list overflowed at 200% text size — fixed in `218b963`.
- Difficulty shown as text only (fine), but no visual scale.

## 5. SEO, AEO and GEO

- `DOMAIN` was empty, so **no canonical URLs, no sitemap.xml, no robots.txt and no og:url /
  og:image** were generated.
- Structured data claimed `"priceRange": "$$"`, which isn't stated anywhere on the site.
- No Person, WebSite or per-trip BreadcrumbList with URLs; FAQPage only on the home page.
- Thin trip pages (about 150 words of unique content each) for queries like
  "Langtang Valley trekking guide".

## 6. Performance (Lighthouse 12.8, mobile, simulated slow 4G, gzip server)

| Page | Perf | LCP | Weight | Requests |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| index.html | 78 | 5.9 s | 1,272 KiB | 13 |
| langtang-valley-trek.html | 77 | 6.7 s | 1,766 KiB | 17 |
| tours.html | 78 | 6.0 s | 1,390 KiB | 13 |

Causes: full-size JPEG heroes and card images with no responsive sizes, 290 KiB of variable
fonts (two preloaded), no image dimensions.

## 7. Business trust and legal

- Legal notice placeholders still open: street address, guide licence number, partner agency
  name and registration, PAN, deposit %, balance timing, payment methods, cancellation tiers,
  complaint reply time, health-data retention (9 placeholders).
- Privacy policy said the site was "hosted on claude.ai by Anthropic" — wrong since the move
  to Vercel.
- FAQ stated guides are mandatory everywhere since 2023; the Everest (Khumbu) region applies
  its own rules.
- Wellness copy said the oxygen checks help "your body adapt safely" — implies a guarantee.
- "Every photo on this website was taken by Jenish" — not verified for the new wildlife photos.
