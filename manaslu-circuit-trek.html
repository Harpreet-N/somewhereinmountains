Manaslu Circuit Trek

A wild, quieter alternative to the Annapurna Circuit around the world's eighth-highest mountain — from green Gurung farmland up through Tibetan-Buddhist villages to the high Larkya La pass.

Highlights

  • Crossing the Larkya La pass with views of Manaslu (8,163 m)
  • Monasteries and mani walls in Samagaun and Lho
  • Restricted-area permit arranged for you

Included with every trek

  • Morning and evening oxygen and heart-rate checks
  • An edited highlight film and your digital trail log
  • A trail comfort pouch with ginger-lemon-honey, masala tea and electrolytes
  • Gear check, SIM and money help the day before
